# PokMarkT
PokMarkT is an Informatics project website designed as a directory than an actual Marketplace.  Important: PokMarkT is not an official Pokémon marketplace and does not sell Pokémon products directly. This website does not hold card inventory, process payments, ship items, or act as a party to transactions between buyers and sellers.
This website does not hold card inventory, accept payments, ship items, or act as a party to transactions between buyers and sellers.

When users click the "View Listing" or "Open Marketplace" button, they will be redirected to a third-party website. Any subsequent transactions take place directly between the user and the respective marketplace or seller.

PokMarkT's Purpose
To help users find Pokémon TCG marketplaces.
To provide basic information on online purchasing safety.
To remind users to conduct research before purchasing cards.
To showcase card examples and direct users to the relevant marketplaces.
Disclaimer: PokMarkT does not guarantee the authenticity, condition, price, availability, or safety of any products or sellers found on third-party marketplaces.
